Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Kay Consult Limited ("we", "us", "our") provides IT consultancy services and software products, including Gadaova. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

Through Gadaova, we collect information event planners provide about themselves and their events (name, email, event details), and information guests provide when responding to an RSVP or purchasing merchandise (name, email, RSVP responses, order details).

Payment information

We do not collect or store card numbers, bank account numbers, or other raw payment credentials. Payments are processed directly by our payment partners — Stripe, Paystack, and PayPal — each governed by their own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, along with a transaction reference.

How we use information

We use collected information to operate the relevant service (for example, running an event's RSVP or merchandise shop), to communicate with users about their account or orders, and to maintain the security and reliability of our products.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information with payment processors (Stripe, Paystack, PayPal) only as needed to process a transaction, and with service providers who help us operate our infrastructure (such as hosting providers), under confidentiality obligations.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the relevant service, or as required by law.
